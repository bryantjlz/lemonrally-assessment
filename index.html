Common Questions

How accurate is this pickleball skill assessment?

Based on USA Pickleball's official skill rating criteria — the same starting point recommended before getting an official DUPR rating.

What's the difference between a 3.0 and a 3.5?

At 3.0 you're consistent with basic shots and understand positioning. At 3.5 you're developing third-shot drops, sustaining dink rallies, and reading your opponent.

Do I need to create an account to take the quiz?

No — the quiz is completely free for Lemon Rally Club members. No sign-up required.

What is pickleball skill assessment used for?

It helps you find the right open play sessions, join the right tournament brackets, and get matched with players who challenge you without overwhelming you.
